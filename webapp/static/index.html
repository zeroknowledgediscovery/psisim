What you are looking at

The state is not one answer per survey item, but a probability distribution over the possible answers of every modelled GSS item: Ψ = (p1, …, pd). Each dot is one item; its colour shows how far its distribution has moved away from the empty-state distribution Ψ0 implied by the trained model when no answers have been supplied.

Faint lines are the learned dependencies of the native LSM: item i is linked to item j when the tree that predicts j uses i.

What an answer does

Answering item i with σ collapses pi to a point mass, propagates the centred response δσ − pi through the learned one-coordinate kernels to every dependent item (the splash), and clamps the answer. The whole state then relaxes under centred finite-n mode sweeps (the ripples). The next question is asked in this new state.

All mathematics runs in the native PsiSim C++ runtime; the browser only draws snapshots.

How not to read it

  • Links are learned statistical dependencies between survey responses. They are not causal links.
  • This is a model of survey-response dependencies, not a picture of a brain.
  • Relaxation sweeps also move the state on their own: Ψ₀ is not a fixed point of the finite-n mode sweeps, so ripples after an answer mix its response with the model's intrinsic relaxation.
  • Positions are a layout for readability. Screen distance and ripple timing do not represent physical propagation time — each ripple is one discrete simulation snapshot.
